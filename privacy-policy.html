Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Bodhika Edutech Pvt. Ltd. ("Bodhika", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Bodhika website and mobile application (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, institute or school name, and class/grade.
  • Usage data: tests attempted, answers submitted, scores, time spent per question, and app interaction logs.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and IP address.
  • Payment information: where applicable, processed securely through third-party payment gateways — Bodhika does not store full card details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including test delivery and result generation.
  • Generate performance analytics and personalized practice recommendations.
  • Communicate updates, exam reminders, and support responses.
  • Improve the Service through aggregated, anonymized usage analysis.
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or misuse.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with:

  • Coaching institutes or schools that have enrolled you on Bodhika, for the purpose of academic reporting.
  • Service providers who help us operate the Service (hosting, analytics, payment processing), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of Bodhika, its users, or the public.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Your Choices

You may access, update, or request deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the details below. You may also opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

Bodhika is used by school and coaching-institute students, including minors. Where a user is under 18, we require that a parent, guardian, or institute has consented to the collection of information as part of enrollment, in accordance with applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the app or via email. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@bodhika.online or write to Bodhika Edutech Pvt. Ltd., Pune, Maharashtra, India.
